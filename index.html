Ask us anything
How long does a full wrap take?

Three to five days for a full colour change on most cars, depending on how much needs stripping and how complex the panels are. Partial wraps and accents are usually same-day or next-day. We'll give you a firm date with the quote.

Will PPF or a wrap damage my paint?

Not on healthy factory paint — both come off cleanly and actually protect what's underneath. The risk is on panels that have already been resprayed badly, where the old paint can lift with the film. We check for that before we start and tell you if it's a concern.

What's the difference between PPF and ceramic coating?

PPF is a physical film that absorbs stone chips and light scratches. Ceramic is a chemical layer that makes the surface slick, easy to wash and more resistant to water spots and fading. They solve different problems, and plenty of people have both — PPF on the front end, ceramic over everything.

Do performance upgrades void my warranty?

Some can, depending on the manufacturer and how far you go. We'll tell you up front which side of that line a job sits on so you can decide with the facts. Plenty of what we do — exhausts, intakes, cosmetic work — doesn't touch it.

Can I see the car while it's being worked on?

Yes. Drop in whenever — it's your car. We also send progress photos on WhatsApp so you don't have to.

Do you only work on high-end cars?

No. The supercars get photographed, but most of what's in the bay on any given day is ordinary daily driven cars getting serviced, wrapped or tidied up. Same standard either way.
